👥 Team Building Tips

Anomaly / Disorder DPS. Hoshimi Miyabi deals damage through elemental anomaly procs rather than raw ATK. Pair her with a second Anomaly agent of a different element for Disorder chains, or a Support/Defense buffer. Typical setup: Anomaly + Anomaly + Support or Anomaly + Stun + Support.

❓ FAQ — Hoshimi Miyabi

Is Hoshimi Miyabi worth pulling?
Hoshimi Miyabi is a strong Ice Anomaly agent. If your roster lacks Ice coverage or a Anomaly role, Hoshimi Miyabi fills that gap well. Check the recommended teams above to see if you have the right teammates.
What stats should I prioritize?
Anomaly Proficiency > ATK% > Element DMG%. Slot 4: ATK% or CRIT. Slot 5: Ice DMG%. Slot 6: Anomaly Mastery or ATK%. Aim for 300+ Anomaly Proficiency.
Best F2P Anomaly W-Engine?
The A-Rank Electro-Lip Gloss is excellent F2P options for Hoshimi Miyabi. Anomaly Proficiency substats provide solid synergy without spending Monochrome.
Which Drive Disc set is best?
For Hoshimi Miyabi, the Branch & Blade Song set (Ice) is generally the strongest choice because the Ice DMG bonus directly amplifies her damage. Mix with a 2-Piece Freedom Blues or Chaos Jazz for the 4+2 setup.
