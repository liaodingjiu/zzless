❓ FAQ — Pan Yinhu

Is Pan Yinhu worth pulling?
Pan Yinhu is a solid A-Rank Defense. A-Ranks are easier to max (cheaper materials, more dupe copies), and a maxed A-Rank often outperforms a base S-Rank. Worth building if you need Physical coverage.
What stats should I prioritize?
DEF% > HP% > ATK%. Slot 4: DEF% or HP%. Slot 5: DEF% or HP%. Slot 6: DEF%. Defense agents scale primarily with DEF or HP depending on their kit.
Best F2P Defense W-Engine?
The A-Rank Big Cylinder and Bunny Band are excellent F2P options for Pan Yinhu. DEF substats provide solid synergy without spending Monochrome.
Which Drive Disc set is best?
For Pan Yinhu, the Fanged Metal set (Physical) is generally the strongest choice because the Physical DMG bonus directly amplifies her damage. Mix with a 2-Piece appropriate set for the 4+2 setup.
