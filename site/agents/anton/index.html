Anton Ivanov is an A-Rank agent available from the standard (Stable) banner and all limited banners. Regular gameplay and events will naturally give you copies. A-Rank agents are cheaper to build and faster to max compared to S-Ranks.

👥 Team Building Tips

Main DPS / Hyper Carry. Anton Ivanov wants to stay on-field dealing damage. Pair her with a Stun agent to build Daze and create Chain Attack windows, and a Support to buff her damage. Typical setup: DPS + Stun + Support.

❓ FAQ — Anton Ivanov

Is Anton Ivanov worth pulling?
Anton Ivanov is a solid A-Rank Attack. A-Ranks are easier to max (cheaper materials, more dupe copies), and a maxed A-Rank often outperforms a base S-Rank. Worth building if you need Electric coverage.
What stats should I prioritize?
CRIT Rate / CRIT DMG > ATK% > Element DMG%. Slot 4: CRIT Rate or CRIT DMG. Slot 5: Electric DMG%. Slot 6: ATK%.
Best F2P Attack W-Engine?
The A-Rank Cannon Rotor and Drill Rig - Red Axis are excellent F2P options for Anton Ivanov. CRIT Rate substats provide solid synergy without spending Monochrome.
Which Drive Disc set is best?
For Anton Ivanov, the Thunder Metal set (Electric) is generally the strongest choice because the Electric DMG bonus directly amplifies her damage. Mix with a 2-Piece Woodpecker Electro or Astral Voice for the 4+2 setup.
