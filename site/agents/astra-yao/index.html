Astra Yao is a Limited S-Rank agent. She appears exclusively on her featured banner during special event periods and is not in the standard (Stable) banner pool. Save your Encrypted Master Tapes and Polychrome for her next rerun.

👥 Team Building Tips

Buffer / Enabler. Astra Yao amplifies your team's damage. She fits into almost any comp — pair her with an Attack or Anomaly DPS who benefits from her buffs, plus a Stun or second Support. Typical setup: DPS + Stun + Support.

❓ FAQ — Astra Yao

Is Astra Yao worth pulling?
Astra Yao is a strong Ether Support agent. If your roster lacks Ether coverage or a Support role, Astra Yao fills that gap well. Check the recommended teams above to see if you have the right teammates.
What stats should I prioritize?
Energy Regen > ATK% or HP%. Slot 4: ATK% or HP%. Slot 5: ATK% or HP%. Slot 6: Energy Regen. Supports scale differently — check specific skill scalings.
Best F2P Support W-Engine?
The A-Rank Bashful Demon is excellent F2P options for Astra Yao. ATK substats provide solid synergy without spending Monochrome.
Which Drive Disc set is best?
For Astra Yao, the Chaotic Metal set (Ether) is generally the strongest choice because the Ether DMG bonus directly amplifies her damage. Mix with a 2-Piece Swing Jazz or Astral Voice for the 4+2 setup.
