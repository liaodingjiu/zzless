❓ FAQ — Starlight Engine

Is Starlight Engine worth pulling?
Starlight Engine is a solid A-Rank Attack W-Engine. At R5 (5 copies), it competes with R1 S-Rank options. Since A-Rank copies accumulate naturally from pulling, this is a reliable long-term investment for your Attack agents.
Should I refine Starlight Engine?
Yes — A-Rank W-Engines are worth refining to R5. The passive scaling per refinement level is significant, and you will naturally pull copies over time.
Best F2P alternative to Starlight Engine?
The A-Rank Cannon Rotor and Drill Rig - Red Axis are excellent free alternatives. CRIT Rate 20% provides solid Attack synergy without spending Polychrome. The Battle Pass Cannon Rotor is also a top-tier option for $10.
Does the passive work on off-type agents?
No. The Cosmic Drive only activates when equipped by a Attack agent. Equipping this on any other Specialty gives only raw ATK + ATK — the passive is completely disabled. Always match W-Engine type to agent Specialty.
