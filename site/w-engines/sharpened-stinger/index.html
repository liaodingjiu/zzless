❓ FAQ — Sharpened Stinger

Is Sharpened Stinger worth pulling?
Sharpened Stinger is a Limited S-Rank Anomaly W-Engine with 713 Base ATK and Anomaly Proficiency 90. It's a strong upgrade for Anomaly agents who need Anomaly Proficiency. If you main a Anomaly carry, it's worth considering — but an R5 A-Rank alternative can come close in performance for much less Polychrome.
Should I refine Sharpened Stinger?
Refining a Limited S-Rank is a luxury, not a necessity. Two agents with R1 S-ranks > one agent with R2. Only refine if you have no other Anomaly agent who needs a strong weapon.
Best F2P alternative to Sharpened Stinger?
The A-Rank Electro-Lip Gloss and Rainforest Gourmet are excellent free alternatives. Anomaly Proficiency 75 provides solid Anomaly synergy without spending Polychrome.
Does the passive work on off-type agents?
No. The Venomous Edge only activates when equipped by a Anomaly agent. Equipping this on any other Specialty gives only raw ATK + Anomaly Proficiency — the passive is completely disabled. Always match W-Engine type to agent Specialty.
