Drive Discs are the equipment system in Zenless Zone Zero — similar to artifacts or relics in other gacha games. Each agent can equip 6 discs across 6 slots. Matching 2 discs from the same set activates a 2-Piece bonus; matching 4 activates a 4-Piece bonus on top of the 2pc effect. The most common setup is 4+2 (one full set for the 4pc effect + one 2pc from another set for extra stats).

What are Drive Discs?

Drive Discs are equipable gear sets in ZZZ. Each set provides a 2-Piece bonus and a 4-Piece bonus. You equip 6 discs (one per slot) on each agent. Bonuses only activate when you equip enough discs from the same set — they don't require specific slot combinations.

How to farm Drive Discs?

Farm discs at Routine Cleanup (Bardic Needle) using Battery Charge, unlocked at Inter-Knot Lv. 20. Higher-difficulty stages yield S-rank discs more often. You can also dismantle unwanted discs at the Music Store and craft specific sets from the materials.

4-Piece vs 2-Piece — which is better?

It depends on the agent. 4-Piece is stronger if the 4pc effect directly synergizes with your agent's kit (e.g. Inferno Metal for Fire DPS). 2+2+2 (three 2pc bonuses) works well when no 4pc effect perfectly matches, giving more flexible stat stacking.

What do the 6 slots do?

Slots 1–3 have fixed main stats: HP (Slot 1), ATK (Slot 2), DEF (Slot 3). Slots 4–6 have random main stats like CRIT Rate, CRIT DMG, Element DMG%, ATK%, and Energy Regen. Slot 4–6 are where most of your farming RNG happens.

Can you mix different Drive Disc sets?

Yes! You can run 4+2 (one full set + one 2pc), 2+2+2 (three 2pc bonuses), or even 4+1+1 if you only need one 4pc. The only restriction is you can't activate more than one 4-Piece bonus at once (that would require 8 slots).
