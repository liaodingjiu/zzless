❓ Quick Answers

What is Zenless Zone Zero?

Zenless Zone Zero (ZZZ) is an urban fantasy action RPG developed by HoYoverse. It features real-time combat, an Agent system with elements and specialties, Bangboo companions, and roguelike Hollow exploration.

How many agents are in ZZZ?

There are 44 playable agents across 5 elements (Fire, Electric, Ice, Physical, Ether) and 6 specialties (Attack, Stun, Anomaly, Support, Defense, Rupture).

What is the best agent in ZZZ?

Top-tier agents include Miyabi (Frost Anomaly), Nangong Yu (Ether Stun), Aria (Ether Attack), Ye Shunguang (Physical Attack), and Sunna (Physical Support). Check our Tier List for complete rankings.

How do I build an agent in ZZZ?

Use our Build Planner to select an agent, equip a W-Engine and 6 Drive Discs, and preview final stats. Match the W-Engine type and choose the right 4+2 Drive Disc combination.

Is Zenless Zone Zero free to play?

Yes! Zenless Zone Zero is completely free to play on PC (Epic Games Store, HoYoPlay), PlayStation 5, iOS, and Android. In-game purchases are optional and cosmetic or convenience-based.

How does the pity / gacha system work?

ZZZ uses a soft pity system. On the character banner, the 5-star rate jumps from 0.6% to ~6.6% starting at pull #74, increasing each pull. ~95% of players get their S-rank by pull 82. The weapon banner soft pity starts at pull #65. If you lose the 50/50, your next S-rank is guaranteed to be the banner agent. Pity carries over between banners of the same type. See our Gacha System Guide for full details.

I’m new — who should I pull?

If you have Pyrois (free S-rank from story), pulling Norma is highly recommended — Pyrois + Norma + Nicole is one of the best low-cost starter teams. Otherwise, wait for the next version livestream to see upcoming banners before spending Polychrome. Supports like Sunna and stunners like Dialyn are always safe investments. Check our Tier List and Beginner’s Guide for more.

What is the best F2P team for beginners?

Pyrois + Norma + Nicole is widely recommended as the best low-cost team. It covers DPS, stun, and support roles with excellent synergy. Another strong F2P option is Soldier 11 + Koleda + Lucy. Focus on one team at first — invest in their levels, W-Engines, and Drive Discs before building a second team.

How much Polychrome can I earn as F2P?

F2P players can earn approximately 12,000–15,000 Polychrome per patch (6 weeks) from daily commissions, Hollow Zero, Shiyu Defense, events, and redeem codes. New players have a massive one-time pool from story, commissions, and achievements — easily 30,000+ Polychrome. That’s enough to guarantee an S-rank agent (requires ~22,000 for hard pity).
