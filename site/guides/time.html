How Much Time Does ZZZ Actually Take?

A realistic breakdown for players considering switching from HSR, WuWa, or Genshin — and for returning players wondering if they can fit ZZZ into their schedule. Based on community-sourced timing data.

Average time per day, spread across a patch cycle:

~8–15 min

Quiet days: 30 seconds. Busy days: 1–2 hours (by choice).

Daily commissions auto-complete as you spend battery. There is no "talk to 3 NPCs" or "collect 5 flowers" — it is purely spend stamina and leave.

🔄 Weekly Tasks

Two things reset each week. Both can be done in one sitting.

Hollow Zero — Lost Void

A themed series of battles with a final boss. You pick buffs between stages (similar to HSR's Divergent Universe, but with way less clicking). New characters on banners are instantly playable here — no timegate. Each run varies by theme; some weeks cap your HP at 30%, forcing cautious play. Substantial rewards.

Lost Void — Operation Matrix
1 run per week for full rewards
3–10m

Notorious Hunt

Three free farming runs per week for Core Skill upgrade materials. Additional runs cost battery. Rewards are small — this is skippable if you are not actively upgrading core skills.

Notorious Hunt ×3

⚔️ Bi-Weekly Endgame (Alternates Each Week)

Deadly Assault and Shiyu Defense reset on alternating schedules. One resets each week, so you always have one endgame mode to do — never both at once.

Deadly Assault

Three boss fights. Characters are locked per boss — once you use an agent on one boss, they cannot be reused. Timed: 3 minutes per attempt. Minimum ~10 minutes for 3 clears. Can easily take an hour if you are restarting or experimenting with teams.

Deadly Assault — 3 bosses
Characters locked per boss. Resets every 2 weeks
10–60m

Shiyu Defense — Critical Node

Up to 5 stages of multi-wave fights. On stage 5, characters AND gear are locked. If you S-ranked the previous cycle, stages 1–3 auto-clear — the shortcut saves significant time.

Shiyu Critical — Stages 4+5
Stages 1–3 auto-clear if you S-ranked last cycle
10–20m

🎪 Events

Event quality in ZZZ is higher than HSR — they feel like actual gameplay, not chores. Time varies widely:

  • Calendar-locked events: One stage per day. Usually <5 min per stage. Some stages take <10 seconds.
  • Small events: Clearable in one sitting. ~15 minutes total.
  • Major events (Pale Wasteland, Beach Episode): Full story + gameplay expansion. Can take hours — but these are the best content in the game and worth every minute.

Major events are the reason people play ZZZ. They are not filler — they are full story arcs with new maps, mechanics, and voice acting.

💡 The Real Answer

Quiet days: 30 seconds to spend battery and leave. That's it.

Normal days: 5–10 minutes — dailies + a Lost Void run or event stage.

Endgame days: 30–60 minutes if you are pushing Deadly Assault scores or experimenting with Shiyu teams — but this is optional. You can clear 80% of rewards in 10 minutes and stop.

Patch launch days: 2–4 hours for the new story chapter and major event. But you will want to play that much — it is the best content ZZZ has.

If you are considering switching from HSR: ZZZ takes less daily time but demands more active attention during endgame. There is no auto-battle. If you enjoy real-time combat, the tradeoff is worth it. If you prefer auto-battle while doing other things, stick with HSR.
