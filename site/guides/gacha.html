Gacha & Pity System Guide

Everything you need to know about ZZZ's gacha mechanics — soft pity, 50/50, hard pity, pull currencies, and how to maximize your Polychrome as a free-to-play player. Based on community-verified data from zzz.rng.moe.

🎰 How the Pity System Works

Zenless Zone Zero uses the same gacha model as other HoYoverse titles (Genshin Impact, Honkai: Star Rail). Every pull on a limited banner has a chance to drop an S-rank agent or W-Engine. The system guarantees you will get one within a certain number of pulls through "pity."

Soft Pity Explained

After a certain number of pulls without an S-rank, your odds increase dramatically each pull. This is called "soft pity" — and it is the real mechanic that determines when you get your S-rank.

Key takeaway: "Hard pity" (90 for character, 80 for weapon) is mostly theoretical. In practice, the overwhelming majority of players get their S-rank between pulls 75–82. Going to 90 is a ~1 in 13 million chance — it essentially never happens.

🎯 The 50/50 System

When you pull an S-rank on a limited character banner, there is a 50% chance it is the featured agent and a 50% chance it is a standard S-rank (the "50/50").

If You Lose the 50/50

  • Your next S-rank on any limited character banner is 100% guaranteed to be the featured agent.
  • This guarantee carries over between limited character banners — it does not expire. If you lose 50/50 on Norma's banner, save your guarantee for Remielle.
  • The guarantee is banner-type specific: character guarantees only apply to character banners, weapon to weapon banners.

If You Win the 50/50

  • Your next S-rank will again be a 50/50 — there is no "win streak" protection.
  • Your pity counter resets to 0 after pulling any S-rank.

📊 F2P Polychrome Income

Per Patch (6 Weeks) — Recurring Sources

  • Daily Commissions: 60/day × 42 days = ~2,520
  • Hollow Zero (weekly): ~640/week × 6 = ~3,840
  • Shiyu Defense (biweekly): 720/cycle × 3 = ~2,160
  • Events (per patch): ~1,500–2,500
  • Redeem Codes: ~300 per patch
  • Inter-Knot Membership ($5/month): +90/day = +3,780 per patch

With $5 monthly pass: ~16,000–19,000 Polychrome (enough for ~100–118 pulls)

One-Time Sources for New Players

  • Main Story + Commissions: ~10,000–15,000
  • Exploration & Achievements: ~5,000–8,000
  • Character Trials & Tutorials: ~2,000–3,000
  • First-Time Clear Rewards: ~3,000–5,000

New player one-time pool: ~30,000+ Polychrome. That is enough to guarantee at least one limited S-rank agent right away.

🧠 Pull Strategy Tips

For Free-to-Play Players

  • Never pull on a banner unless you can guarantee. Going in with less than ~22,000 Polychrome and losing 50/50 leaves you empty-handed.
  • Wait for livestream before pulling. Current banners are always active during the preview. See the next patch's characters before spending on the current one.
  • W-Engine banner is optional. For F2P, A-rank W-Engines and the battle pass weapon are competitive alternatives. Only pull W-Engines if you have spare pulls after securing your target agents.
  • Supports age better than DPS. Top-tier supports (Sunna, Astra Yao, Yuzuha) stay relevant across many team compositions. A new DPS may be powercrept in 3–6 months.
  • Build one team before a second. A hyper-invested 3-agent core can clear most content. Spreading resources across 6+ agents too early slows progress dramatically.

Pity Counter Tracking

You can check your exact pity count in-game from the banner history page. Alternatively, use third-party trackers like zzz.rng.moe (community warp tracker) to log your pulls and visualize your pity progress.
