🏆 Meta Teams (S-Tier)

These are the strongest team compositions in the current meta. They combine top-tier agents with perfect synergy for maximum damage output.

❄️ Miyabi Disorder

Disorder • Dual Anomaly • Ice + Electric
❄️ Miyabi S ⚡ Yanagi S 💪 Sunna S

The undisputed #1 team. Miyabi and Yanagi chain Freeze → Shock → Disorder for massive burst windows. Sunna provides ATK buffs and Physical coverage. This team clears all content effortlessly.

Premium (3× S-Rank)

✨ Ether Dominance

Hyper Carry • Ether • Stun + Support
✨ Aria S ✨ Nangong Yu S ✨ Astra Yao S

Full Ether synergy. Nangong Yu stuns → Aria unloads during Chain Attack windows → Astra Yao buffs both. Corruption DoT adds consistent background damage. Few enemies resist Ether, making this comp universally effective.

Premium (3× S-Rank)

💪 Physical Hyper Carry

Hyper Carry • Physical • DPS + Defense + Support
💪 Ye Shunguang S 💪 Caesar King S 💪 Sunna S

Ye Shunguang deals devastating Physical damage with Caesar King providing shields and Sunna amplifying ATK. Safe and consistent — Caesar King's shields let you facetank while Ye Shunguang tears through enemies.

The #1 F2P recommendation. Pyrois is free from the main story. Norma (Anby) is a starter stunner who builds Daze reliably. Nicole groups enemies and provides DEF shred. All three are given to you — zero pulls needed.

🌀 Disorder Teams (Dual Anomaly)

Disorder is triggered when a second Attribute Anomaly is applied before the first expires. These teams pair two Anomaly agents of different elements for massive chain reactions.

⚡🔥 Electric × Fire Disorder

Disorder • Electric + Fire • Dual Anomaly
⚡ Yanagi S 🔥 Burnice S 🔥 Lucy A

Yanagi builds Electric anomaly rapidly while Burnice applies Burn. Disorder triggers between Shock and Burn for continuous damage spikes. Lucy provides ATK buff for both DPS units.

Premium (2× S-Rank + 1× A-Rank)

💪❄️ Physical × Ice Disorder

Disorder • Physical + Ice • Dual Anomaly
💪 Jane Doe S ❄️ Miyabi S ❄️ Soukaku A

Jane Doe's Assault + Miyabi's Freeze = constant Disorder procs. Soukaku buffs Ice DMG and ATK. Miyabi acts as the primary on-field carry with Jane Doe swapping in to apply Physical anomaly.

🛠️ How to Build a Team in ZZZ

1. Pick your Main DPS. This is your on-field carry who deals the most damage. Choose based on your roster and the enemies' elemental weaknesses.

2. Add a Stunner or Second DPS. For hyper carry teams, a Stunner builds Daze to create Chain Attack windows. For Disorder teams, add a second Anomaly agent of a different element.

3. Add a Support or Flex. Supports buff damage, heal, or provide shields. Defense agents offer survivability for difficult content. Pick what your team lacks.

4. Match elements when possible. Mono-element teams benefit from shared buffs and debuffs. However, a mixed-element team with synergy beats a mono team without it.

5. Use the Build Planner. Once you've picked your team, use the interactive Build Planner to equip W-Engines and Drive Discs for each agent.

❓ Team Building FAQ

Do I need all S-Rank agents for good teams?
No. A-Rank agents at max refinement (R5 W-Engine, C6 Mindscape) can match or exceed base S-Rank performance. The Pyrois + Norma + Nicole F2P team clears all story and most endgame content. S-Ranks give you more options but aren't required.
Should I build mono-element teams?
Mono-element teams are strong when fighting enemies weak to that element. However, Shiyu Defense often features enemies with different weaknesses on each side, so having two teams with different elements is more important than mono-element synergy.
How important is team synergy vs individual strength?
Synergy > individual strength. Three S-Rank agents with no synergy will underperform a well-synergized team with A-Ranks. Buff stacking, elemental reactions, and role balance matter more than raw stats.
What's the best Bangboo for my team?
Match your Bangboo's element to your main DPS. Luckyboo (Physical) and Electroboo (Electric) are versatile picks. Check our Bangboo database for elemental match recommendations.
